Good morning everyone, and thank you for joining the weekly product meeting. Today we will review the launch plan for the new desktop app, the budget for the next quarter, and the results of the customer survey. First, the launch. The team finished the beta last Friday, and we received about two hundred bug reports. Most of them were small, like a button in the wrong place or a slow settings window. Three of them were serious, and we fixed all three on Monday. Second, the budget. We plan to spend less on servers, because the new design sends audio straight to the speech provider. That removes one service we pay for every month. Third, the survey. Seventy percent of customers said they use the app every day, and most of them asked for better search in past meetings. We will look at search after the launch. Are there any questions before we move on? If not, let us talk about the timeline for December, and who will own each part of the release.
